## HOA Compliance Desk
Helps small HOA boards stay on top of paperwork: it works out the send-by date for an annual meeting notice from the notice period your board types in (with the bylaw it came from) and drafts the notice; lists upcoming dues, reserve, and insurance dates with reminder dates; drafts friendly payment reminders; and gives a 14-item records checklist. A Lake Crest HOA demo is included, marked as sample content. Drafts only, nothing is sent. Hides SSN and card numbers, works offline, stores nothing. Not legal advice.
