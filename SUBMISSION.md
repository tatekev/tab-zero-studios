## Unpack
Turns a confusing letter or bill into three plain answers: what it is, what to do, and by when. Flags urgency and gives one tiny first step. Built for brains that freeze at paperwork. Offline, stores nothing. Not legal, tax, or financial advice.

## CPA Doc Prep
A tax-document checklist and tracker for employees, investors, self-employed people, parents, and homeowners. Marks what's received or missing and drafts an email or text to your preparer. Warns before full SSNs or card numbers get pasted. Organizes paperwork only; no tax advice.

## HOA Board Desk
Helps HOA boards turn outdated info into a clean, accessible site, FAQ, and monthly newsletter. Flags stale dates and placeholder text, cites sources in the FAQ, and drafts everything for board review. Not legal advice.
