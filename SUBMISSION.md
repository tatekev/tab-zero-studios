## Unpack
Turns a confusing letter or bill into three plain answers: what it is, what to do, and by when. Rates urgency, gives one tiny first step, and says so when no deadline is found (it never invents one). English letters only. Hides SSN and card numbers, works offline, stores nothing. Not legal, tax, or financial advice.

## CPA Doc Prep
A tax-document checklist and tracker for employees, investors, self-employed people, parents, and homeowners. Tracks what's received, missing, or sent, and drafts an email or text to your preparer (you press send). Checks text for full SSNs or card numbers and refuses to put them in a draft. The checklist is a general starting list, not complete. Organizes paperwork only; never computes tax or gives advice.

## HOA Board Desk
Helps HOA boards turn outdated info into a clean 3-page site, FAQ, and monthly newsletter. Flags past dates and placeholder text (like TBD or coming soon) for the board to check, shows the sources you give for each FAQ answer (and labels answers with none), and drafts everything for board review. The site follows basic accessibility practices (page language, headings, alt text, contrast). Not legal advice.
