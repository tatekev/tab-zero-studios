## Caregiver Binder
A fill-in "if something happens" binder for caregivers and families: which accounts and documents exist and where they are, who to call first, and a 14-step first-72-hours checklist. It records locations, never secrets: it refuses passwords, PINs, recovery codes, SSNs, card numbers, and long account numbers. Finds gaps (no backup contact, no health directive listed) and prints a clean binder. Works offline, stores nothing. Not legal, medical, or financial advice.
