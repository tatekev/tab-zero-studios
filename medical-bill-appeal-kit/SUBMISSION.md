## Medical Bill & Appeal Kit
Helps you check a medical bill and fight a denial: a 12-step bill checklist, a draft letter asking for an itemized bill, a draft appeal letter built only from your own words, and a deadline tracker. A deadline is saved only if you say where on your letter you found it (it never guesses). Drafts only, nothing is sent. Hides SSN and card numbers, works offline, stores nothing. Not medical, legal, or insurance advice.
