## Lease-End Deposit Packet
Helps renters get a deposit back: a move-in/move-out photo log with a side-by-side comparison, a draft demand letter built from your own reasons, and a deadline tracker that uses rules you type in (with where you found them). It doesn't know or state any state's law and never guesses a deadline. Drafts only, nothing is sent. Hides SSN and card numbers, works offline, stores nothing. Not legal advice.
