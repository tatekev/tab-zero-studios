## Tax Notice Helper
Turns an IRS or state tax letter into a plain answer, what to do, and a reply checklist. Shows a reply-by date only when one is written in the letter (never invents one), flags scam signs and strict-deadline words, and says so when a question asks whether to pay or dispute. English letters only. Hides SSN and card numbers, works offline, stores nothing. Not legal or tax advice.
