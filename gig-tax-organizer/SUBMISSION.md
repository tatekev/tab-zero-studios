## Gig Tax Organizer
Organizes self-employed and gig-work records: quarterly date reminders (the usual dates are shown as suggestions to confirm), a mileage log, a receipt log, and a year-end packet to hand your tax preparer. Finds gaps like missing receipt photos or double entries, and imports/exports CSV. It adds up only what you typed and never computes tax, deductions, or what you owe. Hides SSN and card numbers, works offline, stores nothing. Not tax advice.
